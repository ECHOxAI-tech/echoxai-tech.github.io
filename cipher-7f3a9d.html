The Alexander Cipher

A novel — Eko Svenningsson

v165~91,000 wordsEnglishComplete
At 8:56 PM on Thursday, October 30, 2025, the Museum kept different hours than the world outside. Official closing was six. After that, the building continued on its own terms — security lights, climate systems, soft relays clicking in the walls. A controlled afterlife.

Emma Voss restores objects whose histories have been deliberately interrupted. Miles Ashford understands buildings by the pressure they conceal. A dead man leaves each of them evidence that should belong to the other, and an ancient object begins behaving less like an artefact than an invitation.

The Alexander Cipher moves through museums, archives, private foundations, and the quieter architecture of dependence. It is a thriller about what institutions can hide in plain sight—and what happens when two people stop accepting the record they were given.

The full manuscript remains private and is available by request.
