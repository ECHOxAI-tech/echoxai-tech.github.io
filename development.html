Interactive web system

The Dark Hierarchy Tools

This six-part interactive system is a digital extension of the book The Dark Hierarchy: A Treatise on Authority, Initiation, and the Order. The book establishes the concepts; the tools let a reader enter that architecture through structured reflection, individual results, and comparison.

Each tool translates a defined part of the book into an experience rather than reducing the work to a summary. Together they generate readable interpretations and visual result cards, create six-character retrieval codes, and allow several results to be assembled into one profile. Two people can also compare profiles without creating accounts.

For whom

For readers who want to enter the ideas

Readers, couples, and reflective practitioners who want to test concepts against lived experience rather than receive a simplified summary of the source work.

Why now

A book can become an instrument

The browser makes a private, account-free bridge between long-form theory and individual reflection possible—without turning the work into a diagnostic service.

Connected results

Each result receives a retrieval code. With explicit consent, codes can be reopened on another device, assembled into a layered profile, or compared between two people.

Privacy by choice

Local-only storage is available without remote transmission. Cross-device profile storage is separately explained and enabled only by the visitor.

Generated artifacts

Several tools render individual maps and result cards directly in the browser for saving and sharing.

Enter the interactive system

These tools are instruments for personal reflection. They do not provide psychological, therapeutic, clinical, or medical advice, and no professional relationship is formed by using them.
