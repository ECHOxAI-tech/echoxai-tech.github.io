The premise

The physical deck remains central

Most tarot applications begin by replacing the practitioner’s deck. ECHOxLUMINA begins with the opposite assumption: the deck, spread, and established practice already matter. A photograph of a physical spread becomes the starting point for recognition, confirmation, and a reading shaped by a consistent interpretive framework.

The application is intended for practitioners who want continuity between physical ritual and digital convenience without reducing the experience to generic card definitions.

Designed experience

Recognition, interpretation, and return

Deck-aware recognition

Photograph a spread or use an existing image. Recognised cards, positions, and orientations are presented for confirmation before interpretation.

A coherent reading voice

Readings are generated through one defined practitioner framework, with cards interpreted in relation to their position and to one another.

Several ways to practise

Planned spreads range from a single card and three-card reading to the Celtic Cross, relationship, decision, and year-ahead formats.

History and export

Past readings can be revisited, favourited, deleted, and exported in a formatted form rather than disappearing after one session.

Trust and disclosure

Reflection without false certainty

AI-supported recognition and reading generation will be clearly disclosed. The user confirms recognised cards before a reading is created, and the experience is framed as reflective guidance rather than factual prediction, medical advice, legal advice, or a substitute for professional care.

Photography, saved readings, accounts, payments, and deletion controls remain subject to privacy review, store compliance, and release testing before publication.

Release position

The target is February 2027. Final scope, pricing tiers, recognition performance, and optional consultation availability remain subject to validated builds and platform review.
