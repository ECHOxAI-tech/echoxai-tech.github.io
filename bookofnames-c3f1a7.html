The Book of Names

A completed poetry collection — Eko Svenningsson

v137EnglishBerlinComplete
A name is not neutral. It is a tool. It can be a handle, a hook, a receipt — proof that you were counted and therefore manageable. This book is not a confession. It is version control.

Each name in this collection appears twice. The long form remembers atmosphere, sequence, negotiation—the machinery by which experience becomes a story one can live with. The short form keeps only what cannot be explained away. One record accumulates. The other cuts.

Across family, institutions, lovers, mirrors, classrooms, and documents, a person is repeatedly made legible at the cost of being known. The Book of Names follows the intelligence required to survive that process without mistaking survival for consent. It is a ledger, a refusal, and an act of authorship reclaimed from the record.

The complete collection remains private and is available for publication inquiries.
