The Book of Names

A completed poetry collection — Eko Svenningsson

v137EnglishBerlinComplete
A name is not neutral. It is a tool. It can be a handle, a hook, a receipt — proof that you were counted and therefore manageable. This book is not a confession. It is version control.

The Book of Names is built around a single formal principle: each named subject appears twice. The long story tracks what occurred — the sequence, atmosphere, and negotiation. The short story records what remains when explanation is removed. One accumulates. One reduces. One attempts coherence. The other fixes it.

The collection follows what happens when a person is repeatedly misfiled — by family, institutions, and desire — and how the self adapts by performing coherence. Its subjects move through ordinary systems: mirrors, classrooms, lovers, documents. The violence is subtle because it is procedural. The poems study what can be held inside structure without being erased, and what must be carried elsewhere until it is safe enough to breathe.

The complete collection remains private and is available for publication inquiries.
