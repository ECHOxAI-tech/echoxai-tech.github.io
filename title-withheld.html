A contemporary pop-art work

The work

The title, lyrics, audio, and submission details are withheld while the submission process remains active. What can be stated publicly is the artistic ground from which the work emerged.

The piece belongs to the imaginative orbit of Harry Martinson’s Aniara: a work shaped by displacement, irreversible departure, and what remains of humanity when the structures that once gave life direction can no longer be recovered. It approaches that territory through contemporary pop form rather than retelling Martinson’s poem.

Related work

The same constellation of questions is developed in ECHOx’s dramatic work Aniara: The Doors to the Stars. The dramatic work and this music project are separate pieces, but they share an artistic source and a concern with what people carry when return is no longer possible.

Collaboration

ECHOxPRODUCTION

The work is produced in collaboration with ECHOxPRODUCTION, a British-French artist and producer based in London. His practice spans pop and dance production, including official remixes for internationally released artists. echoxstudios.art
