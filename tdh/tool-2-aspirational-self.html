The book argues that desire is the confirmation of a specific self — the sexual persona you most fully want to inhabit. It may precede language, or be discovered over time, or be consciously chosen and refined. What matters is not its origin but its function: it is the self the right person lets you feel, completely, without translation.

This tool gives you the architecture to describe it. Not what acts you want — who you want to be. Some are oriented toward holding, some toward yielding; the orientation is not gendered, and neither pole is higher than the other. Fourteen prompts, mostly open text. You write; the tool assembles your answers into a single profile you can keep.
