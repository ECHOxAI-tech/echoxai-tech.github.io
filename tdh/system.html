The book’s central claim

The book

Complete, structured, and ready to read

The Dark Hierarchy is a finished work of erotic philosophy, speculative theology, and field testimony. It challenges the Lacanian doctrine of lack from inside lived hierarchical practice, and argues that desire can be affirmative and structural rather than an eternal reach toward an absent object.

Written from inside the leather and hierarchical scenes of Berlin, Stockholm, and Amsterdam, it holds sustained argument, liturgy, testimony, and practical reflection in one deliberately authored structure. Its distinction is formal, not a claim to be the first serious book on its subject.

The argument

Four ideas an editor can hold on to

I

The Aspirational Sexual Self

Desire as recognition of the self one may become in another’s presence: an affirmative account set against lack.

II

The Trigger Gradient

Four channels of response (visual, emotional, tactile, intellectual) offered as a reflective framework, not a diagnosis.

III

The Two Neighbors of God

Bergman’s dictum extended: Order and Humor. Order gives a dynamic its spine; humor keeps it from curdling into tyranny.

IV

Voluntary, Revocable Custody

Autonomy as the power to refuse and revise; exclusivity as one chosen, revocable form of belonging.

A reader can try an instrument in minutes, share an anonymous comparison link, or scan the QR on page 312. A journalist can do the same, and the author, the data position and the programming formats are ready.

Responsible by design

The boundaries are stated, not implied

  • Adults only: an 18+ confirmation gates every instrument.
  • Local-first: results stay in the reader’s browser unless they choose cross-device retrieval with a private code.
  • No analytics: the public client sends no result statistics, and sets no marketing tracking cookies.
  • Non-diagnostic: reflection inside a philosophical framework, never clinical measurement. No census or research claim is made.
  • An optional, anonymous, aggregate-only research channel is built but switched off pending ethics review; the thesis is stated as six testable hypotheses.
