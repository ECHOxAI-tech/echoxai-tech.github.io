Six story angles for press

  • “Against Lacan, from inside the dungeon.” A theory book that tests its argument against lived practice rather than the clinic.
  • A book with a free reflection service. Seven instruments, a choice between this device only and a code that works anywhere, no analytics or tracking cookies, an 18+ gate. The only return asked is anonymous, rounded counters for research that identify no one.
  • The author audits himself. The book is “not a confession; it is version control,” and its twentieth movement analyses the author’s own relationship record.
  • “God has two neighbors: Order and Humor.” Bergman’s dictum turned into a household philosophy: quotable, funny, serious.
  • Berlin as a leather capital. A natural partner for Folsom Europe week and the city’s fetish and queer cultural calendar.
  • Consent as theology. Revocability and autonomy are stated as preconditions on page one, answering the “is this safe?” question before it is asked.

What the product team can expect

Is the manuscript finished?

Yes. Release edition v124, 396 pages, 0 errors, 0 undefined references. Full PDF on request. Neither DSM-5 nor ICD-11 counts consensual practice as a disorder.6

Is there a digital product to launch with?

Yes, live at echoxstudios.art/tdh: seven instruments, shareable partner comparison, private retrieval codes, optional emailed results.

Is it responsibly built?

Adults only; the reader chooses between a code that works on any device (explicit consent), this device only, or nothing saved; no marketing cookies; no result statistics sent for analytics; non-diagnostic by design.

What we do not claim. No clinical validity. No sales forecast. No claim that the Echo-System has collected research data, or that the reference overlay is a census. Third-party figures describe the wider field, not this title’s expected reach.
