Desire is not what you feel for someone.
It is what you feel about yourself, in their presence.

The Dark Hierarchy — Book II

Most people understand desire as something they feel toward another person. The book argues it is something else: the confirmation, in another's presence, of the specific version of yourself you most want to be. What we call attraction is that confirmation arriving. The channels through which it arrives — visual, emotional, tactile, intellectual — are your personal trigger gradient.

These tools map that gradient, help you describe the self being confirmed, and give language to the patterns you already carry. Free, no account, and a clear choice between local-only results and optional cross-device storage. Start anywhere.

Results describe patterns for self-understanding only. If you are experiencing distress, please seek support from a qualified professional.

The Dark Hierarchy is a five-book work of philosophy and theology — a complete account of desire, authority, and the hierarchical orientation, written from inside the framework it describes. It dismantles the Lacanian model of desire, builds a new one from the ground up, and follows it all the way into daily practice.
