The argument

A book that can be tested, not only read.

The Dark Hierarchy is a theory of recognition-dependent desire. It does not stop at assertion. It says desire arrives through four channels, that those channels form a gradient and not a set of types, and that in every case it could examine the structure does not depend on the gender of the object. These are claims a research group can test, and the book says plainly that “no exception it could document” is not the same as “no exception”.

The seven reader instruments are built on that theory. They give a reader a private reflection today. They are also designed so that, with consent and ethics approval, they can become a validated measure tomorrow.

Six hypotheses

The roadmap

Three phases, each with a gate.

Phase 0 · now

Reflective instruments Live

Seven instruments with face validity. Local-first by default. No research claim is made.

Phase 1

Anonymous aggregate contribution Built · switched off

An optional, separate, default-off choice to contribute a bucketed summary of a result. It gives descriptive distributions and a feasibility signal for H2. It switches on only after documented ethics review.

Phase 2

Institutional co-project Seeking partner

A separately consented, pre-registered study under a university or research institute’s ethics approval, with item-level data and, where needed, pseudonymised retest linkage. This is where H1 and H3–H6 are tested.

Phase 3

Publication and replication

Pre-registered results with open materials. Null and contrary findings are published.

Anonymous by design

What the research channel can and cannot hold.

  • It stores counters only: tool, dimension, a 10-point bucket and the calendar month. There is no row per person, so individual answers cannot be reconstructed.
  • It holds no retrieval code, e-mail, IP address, device identifier, cookie, free text or demographic.
  • Results from tools 5 and 7, which produce free-text protocols, never contribute.
  • Nothing is published for any tool with fewer than 30 contributions.
  • It is a separate, default-unticked choice, independent of storage and of age confirmation. Because the data are anonymous, a past contribution cannot be located or withdrawn, and the consent text says so.
  • Contributors are self-selected. Results are never presented as prevalence or as clinical measures.

Cloudflare processes IP addresses transiently at its edge to deliver requests; the service does not store them. Full protocol, hypotheses and governance gates are in docs/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md and the data-protection assessment in docs/DPIA.md in the site repository.

For institutions

What is offered, and what is asked.

Offered

A theory, a text and a pipeline

A 381-page work with a full apparatus; seven instruments with documented item logic; a privacy-hardened data pipeline; a ready route to participants among readers; credit and co-authorship on terms agreed in advance.

Asked

Rigour and sponsorship

An ethics sponsor, a methodological lead, access to established comparison measures, and a commitment to pre-registration and open reporting.
