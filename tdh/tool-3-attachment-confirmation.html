Attachment theory describes the symptom. The trigger theory describes the mechanism.

What looks like neediness is actually precision. What looks like withdrawal is the result of asking for confirmation in a register the available frameworks could not deliver.

This tool maps your attachment behaviour not as pathology but as a precise report — about how acutely you read confirmation signals, which direction you move when they go quiet, how reliably your history has matched your need, and how specifically you need to be seen.

This tool does not categorize you. It describes your wiring as information.
