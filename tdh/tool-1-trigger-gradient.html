Desire arrives as confirmation — the experience of feeling like the version of yourself you most want to be, in the presence of another person. That confirmation can arrive through four channels: Visual (appearance, form), Emotional (connection, relational atmosphere), Tactile (touch, physical presence), or Intellectual (shared sexual understanding, being recognised). Most people have all four active — but in different proportions.

This tool maps your personal gradient across the four channels and shows which ones carry most of the confirmatory weight for you. It also compares your distribution to the culturally produced average, which heavily overweights the visual channel at the expense of the rest.

No reading of the book is required. Answer for yourself as you actually are.
